Just a quick note to say that [PGXN Manager] has been updated with more secure
password storage. Existing passwords are unmodified, but the next time you
change your password, it will be upgraded to a more robust password hashing
algorithm that's more resistant to attacks. I recommend everyone update their
passwords. If you have a PGXN Manager account, just hit [password reset],
enter your username or email address, and check your mail for a reset link.
Once you update your password, it will be stored in the new, more secure
format.



  [PGXN Manager]: https://manager.pgxn.org/
  [password reset]: https://manager.pgxn.org/account/forgotten
